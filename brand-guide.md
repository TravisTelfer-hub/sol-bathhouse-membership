# Brand guide: SOL Elements (landing page build, 7 Nov 2026)

Composed from `../../brand/brand.md` (brand-onboarding, 7 Nov 2026) and the live Webflow stylesheet. The brand folder is the source of truth; this file is the working subset for the page.

## Colours
- Ground: #160504 (site body background, near black espresso). Secondary dark panel: #1f0c08. Warm cream panel for contrast moments: #f3efed (the site's off white text stroke).
- Primary accent: #e36b3d terracotta (labels, rules, primary buttons).
- Accent gradient (wordmark, display numerals, button hover): linear-gradient(107deg, #ec7b62 4%, #faaf7f 34%, #e98555 67%, #cb5332 100%).
- Peach highlight: #faaf7f. Deep terracotta: #cb5332, #d15d39. Link underline: #f09565.
- Text on dark: #ffffff headings, rgba(255,255,255,0.8) body, rgba(255,255,255,0.6) captions.
- Text on cream: #160504 headings, #3a2a25 body.

## Type
- Display and headings: Ogg Roman 400 (self hosted TTF from the client site). H1 clamp 2.6rem to 5rem, line height 1.05 to 1.15, letter spacing -0.01em. Uppercase statement lines at 0.02em tracking. No bold weight exists; emphasis comes from size and the gradient.
- Body: Gotham Book 400 (self hosted), 16 to 18px, line height 1.7. Gotham Medium 500 for buttons and labels. Gotham Light 300 for large quiet numerals if needed.
- Labels: Gotham Medium, 0.72 to 0.8rem, uppercase, 0.18em tracking, terracotta.
- Fallbacks: Playfair Display and Montserrat are NOT loaded; system fallbacks are Georgia and system-ui. The brand files ship in `fonts/`.

## Imagery
- Editorial, warm, low light. Dusk over Lotus Lake, charred timber, salt glow, fire, steam, rainforest. People in robes or bathing, never posed to camera.
- Site images are AVIF; converted to JPEG at max 2000px for this build. Originals requested from the client.
- Press photography credit: Timothy Birch (Urban List). Six JPGs from the Tamborine Mountain Glades SOL page are SOL supplied campaign shots (June 2026 set); confirm usage rights with the client before paid traffic.

## Voice
- Sensory, unhurried, second person. Elemental vocabulary (Earth, Air, Fire, Water). Ritual words: soak, breathe, unplug, reset, return.
- "SOL" always upper case. Wordplay allowed in product names only. Sign off "See you at SOL."
- No therapeutic or medical claims (see claims.md). No "detox". Adults only, phone free.
- Australian English. Sentence level " - " never an em dash.
