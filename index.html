The place

Built by hand on a lake, by two people who moved to the mountain.

Russ and Shae Raven moved to Tamborine Mountain, fell for the mist in the trees, and spent four years building SOL: a circular bathhouse over the water, inspired by the Japanese idea of Wabi Sabi. Russ learned the art of Yakisugi from master craftsman Kenji Nishishita and charred the cedar walls himself. The onyx, fossil coral stone and raw timber were chosen by hand.

Every product used is organic, from the Earth Lab scrubs and the teas to the laundry. The water runs through ozone and UV purification. And the whole place is phone free, because presence was the point.

SOL is quite literally, our heart and soul.

Russell Raven, co founder

Is this you?

Who the membership is for, and who it isn't.

This is for you if

  • You live within an hour of Tamborine Mountain and a weekly drive into the rainforest sounds like the point, not the problem.
  • You have been to SOL, or a city bathhouse, and left saying you need to do this more often.
  • You want a ritual you keep, not a treat you ration.
  • You like the idea that Saturday costs the same as Wednesday.

It is not for you if

  • You come to the hinterland once a year. Book a casual session instead; we will see you then.
  • You are pregnant. The pools, saunas and steam rooms are not permitted during pregnancy; the Alchemy Spa has pregnancy safe treatments for you instead.
  • You are under 18. SOL is an adults only sanctuary.
